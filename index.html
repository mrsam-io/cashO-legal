What is Casho (كاشو)?

Casho (كاشو) is a live-shopping app and marketplace in Saudi Arabia. Sellers go live on video from their phones and show the real item. Buyers watch, ask questions in the chat and buy in one tap. On Casho you can buy items, cars and properties, and bid in live auctions, with secure payment inside the app. Casho works in Arabic and English.

  3. 03

    Check your delivery details, see the total with shipping, and pay by card or mada.

  4. 04

    Follow the order from payment to your door, with a chat open to the seller.

Safe buying, built in

  • Pay inside the app

    You pay in the app by card or mada. Your card details never reach the seller.

  • Payment held safely

    Casho holds your payment until you receive the order and the 7-day report period ends.

  • Refund if it is not shipped

    If the seller does not ship within 5 days of payment, the order is cancelled and you are refunded in full.

  • Reviewed sellers

    We review new sellers before they can go live. See something wrong? Report it in the app.

  • Get paid to your bank account and manage your orders in one place.

Questions people ask about Casho

What is Casho (كاشو)?

Casho (كاشو) is a live-shopping marketplace in Saudi Arabia. Sellers go live on video, buyers watch, ask questions and buy in one tap. You can buy items, cars and properties, and bid in live auctions.

Is Casho on the App Store?

Not yet. Casho is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone, and Android is planned next. We will announce it on this page.

How do I buy on Casho?

Watch a live or open the Shop, tap Buy on an item, check your delivery details and pay by card or mada. You can watch without an account. To buy, chat or follow you sign in with an email code or Google.

Is my payment safe?

You pay inside the app, and your card details never reach the seller. Casho holds your payment until you receive the order and the 7-day report period ends. If the seller does not ship within 5 days of payment, you are refunded in full.

Can I buy cars and properties on Casho?

Yes. You can browse cars, bikes, apartments and villas from sellers on Casho, and bid in live auctions.

How do I sell on Casho?

Open Profile in the app and choose Become a seller. We review new sellers before they can go live. Then you go live from your phone, show your products and get paid to your bank account.

Where does Casho deliver, and in which currency?

Delivery is inside Saudi Arabia only, and prices are in Saudi riyals (SAR).

Does Casho work in Arabic?

Yes. Casho works fully in Arabic and in English.

How do I contact Casho?

Email support@appcasho.com. We reply within 2 business days.
