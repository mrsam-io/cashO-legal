Connect Casho to Claude and ChatGPT

You can connect your Casho account to Claude or ChatGPT and ask questions about your own account in plain words: your orders, your sales, your wallet, the sellers you follow and the items you saved. It is optional, free and you can disconnect at any time.

What you can ask

  • "Which of my orders have not been delivered yet?" or "What is the tracking number of my last order?"
  • "How much did I sell this week, and which orders do I still need to ship?" (sellers)
  • "Which auctions end in the next 24 hours?"
  • "Is anyone I follow live right now?"
  • "Search Casho for an Audi A3, a villa or a smart watch under 1,000 SAR."
  • "What does my wallet show for the last 30 days?"

What it can change

Only three small things, and only when you ask for them in the chat: follow or unfollow a seller, save or unsave an item, and schedule a live for a time you choose. Each change is written in a short log inside your account.

What it can never do

  • It cannot place or pay for an order, change a price, move money, or change your account details.
  • It cannot see your phone number, delivery address, bank details or card details.
  • It can only see your own account. It cannot read other people's accounts or private chats.

How to connect

Claude

  1. Open Claude, go to Settings and then Connectors, and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Casho and paste this address: https://api.appcasho.com/functions/v1/ai-connect
  3. Press Connect. A Casho page opens: sign in with the same email or Google account you use in Casho, then press Allow.

ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT settings and the connectors or apps section (turn on developer mode if your plan asks for it), and add a new connector.
  2. Name it Casho, paste the same address, and choose OAuth sign-in.
  3. Sign in on the Casho page that opens and press Allow.

In the Casho app you can also open Profile > Connect your AI to copy the address and see the steps.

Disconnect

Open Profile > Connect your AI in the Casho app and press Disconnect next to the assistant. You can also remove the connector in Claude's or ChatGPT's own settings. After that the assistant cannot read your account any more.

Privacy and safety

  • The assistant only receives the answers to the questions you ask. Casho does not read your chats with the assistant.
  • Connecting is a choice. Use only assistants you trust: what they do with the answers is governed by their own terms and privacy policy.
  • Details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
  • Casho may limit or switch off the connector to protect users.
